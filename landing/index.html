FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is TenderMaster for?

EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) and contracting firms in Pakistan that bid on public-sector tenders and want to qualify faster, submit cleaner packages, and stop missing deadlines.

Does it submit the tender for me?

TenderMaster prepares everything — the eligibility verdict, the checklist, and a finished, correctly-ordered submission PDF. You review and submit it. You always stay in control of the final bid.

How does it know if we're eligible?

It reads the tender's requirements and checks them against your company profile — PEC category, turnover, experience, registrations and more — then gives a clear verdict with the exact reasons.

What if we're missing a document?

The checklist flags it in red. You can upload it, reuse one already in your vault, or ask the AI to explain how to obtain it — or draft it for you, such as affidavits and cover letters.

Is our company data secure?

Yes. Your documents are your firm's most sensitive papers, and they're treated that way — kept private to your account with security and access controls as a core part of the product.
