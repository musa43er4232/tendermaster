# TenderMaster

**The AI that reads tenders, tells you if you qualify, and builds your submission for you.**

For EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction) companies in Pakistan.

---

## The problem

Winning government tenders is slow and risky for EPC firms:

- **Tenders are huge.** 150–300 pages of legal and technical language. A senior engineer spends hours just finding the parts that matter.
- **Eligibility is unclear.** Firms often spend days preparing a bid before realising they don't even qualify (wrong PEC category, missing experience, etc.).
- **One missing document = rejection.** A single missing affidavit, an expired licence, or a wrong document order, and the whole bid is thrown out — before the price is ever read.
- **No memory.** Firms re-type the same company details into every bid, and forget what they bid, won, or lost.
- **Deadlines slip.** Submission dates, bid-security expiry, and licence renewals are tracked on paper or in someone's head.

The result: firms bid on fewer tenders than they could, and lose winnable ones to clerical mistakes.

---

## What TenderMaster does

You set it up **once**, then every tender becomes simple.

**1. One-time setup.** You tell TenderMaster everything about your company — registrations, licences, past projects, certificates, financials, key staff, equipment. This becomes your company's permanent profile. A progress score shows how "tender-ready" you are.

**2. Upload a tender.** Drop in the tender PDF. The AI reads all 200 pages in about a minute and gives you a plain-language summary of what it actually wants.

**3. Get a clear verdict.** TenderMaster tells you, instantly and in plain words:
- ✅ **Eligible** — you can bid.
- ⚠️ **Eligible with gaps** — you qualify, but you're missing 3 documents (here they are).
- ❌ **Not eligible** — and exactly why (e.g. "needs PEC category C-4, you hold C-5").

No more wasting days on bids you can't win.

**4. Get a smart checklist.** A tidy checklist of every document this tender needs. Items you already have show **green** (pulled from your profile). Missing or expired items show **red**, each with an upload button and an **"Ask AI to help me get this"** option that explains how to obtain it or drafts it for you (affidavits, cover letters, etc.).

**5. Get your finished submission.** One click produces a single, correctly-ordered, professional PDF with all required documents — ready to submit. It can even add an **"extra strengths" section**: things about your company that weren't required but help you win.

**6. It remembers and reminds.** TenderMaster tracks every live tender, nudges you before deadlines ("submission in 48 hours", "your bid security expires in 5 days", "your licence renews next month"), and keeps a full history of every tender you've bid on and how it went.

## Use cases

**"Should we even bid on this?"**
A firm gets a tender notice. Instead of a 2-day review, they upload it and get a Go / No-Go answer in two minutes — and skip the ones they can't win.

**"Make sure we don't get rejected on a technicality."**
Before submitting, the firm checks the green/red checklist. TenderMaster catches that their PEC licence expires before the project ends and that they forgot the non-blacklisting affidavit — both fixed before submission instead of after rejection.

**"Build the bid package fast."**
A deadline is two days away. The firm fills the few missing items and clicks generate — the full, correctly-ordered submission PDF is ready in minutes instead of a late-night scramble.

**"Bid on more tenders with the same team."**
Because qualifying and compiling are now fast, a firm that used to handle 4 tenders a month can pursue 12 — without hiring.

**"Stop missing deadlines."**
An office manager relies on TenderMaster's reminders so no submission date, security expiry, or licence renewal is ever missed again.

**"Keep our company knowledge in one place."**
A new staff member can prepare a bid on day one, because every certificate, project record, and document already lives in the company's TenderMaster profile.

**"Learn from the past."**
Before bidding for a particular agency, the firm reviews their history of past bids to that agency — what they submitted, what won, what lost.

## Why it's hard to copy

A look-alike website is easy. What makes TenderMaster defensible is what builds up over time:

- **Your company's data lives inside it.** Once you've loaded years of projects, certificates and records, switching to anything else means starting over.
- **It understands Pakistani tendering deeply.** PPRA/PEC rules, category limits, agency-specific forms and document quirks are built in — not something a generic tool has.
- **It gets smarter with every tender.** It learns how each government agency words its requirements and which documents they always demand. A copycat starts from zero.
- **It becomes your system of record.** Your entire tender history, document vault, and deadline calendar live here. It becomes the place you work from.

The longer a firm uses it, the harder it is to leave — and the harder it is for anyone to clone.
